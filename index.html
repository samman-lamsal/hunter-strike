HOW TO PLAY

🖱 MOVE — Click & hold anywhere; your assassin walks toward the cursor. Release to stop.

🔦 AVOID the red vision cones — guards spot you and open fire.

❤ HIT POINTS — You can take a few hits before going down. Unlock new assassins for more HP.

🗡 STEALTH KILL — Approach a guard from behind for a silent takedown.

💨 SMOKE BOMB — Press Q or SPACE to cloak for 3 seconds.

🗡 THROW KNIFE — Press E or F to launch a knife forward.

⚡ DASH — Double-click anywhere to burst forward.

⏸ PAUSE — Press ESC or P.

💎 COLLECT gems from fallen guards to unlock new assassins.

✓ CLEAR all guards on the level to win!
